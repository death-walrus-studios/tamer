# Changelog

## 0.3.1

- Added beasts that teach Swipe, Web, Savage Rend, Mine!, Trickster's Dance, Furious Howl, Prowl and Screech, from Forever's own data.
- Added Thunderstomp rank 4.

## 0.3.0

- Added a Pet Trainer card for new ranks a Pet Trainer has for your pet, and a chat line when it gets new ones.
- Added Forever's new pet abilities, such as Swipe, Web and Savage Rend, and the Fox family.
- Tamer now picks up pet abilities it doesn't know yet from your pet's spellbook and Beast Training.
- Ranks you buy from a Pet Trainer now count right away.
- With no pet out, the window shows Call Pet, and its button calls your pet.

## 0.2.0

- Redesigned the window with big icons and three cards: learn now, coming soon, and go find these. Details are in tooltips.
- Added a Teach button that opens Beast Training.
- Fixed the minimap icon being see-through.
- Fixed the level-up line showing after a reload.
- Restart the game after updating.

## 0.1.1

- Fixed Tamer not seeing Beast Training on Forever.
- Fixed Tamer not reading your pet's abilities and training points on Forever.
- Added /tamer debug, which prints what the game returns for bug reports.

## 0.1.0

- First test version.
- Shows what your pet knows, what it can train now, what's next, and which beasts teach the ranks you're missing.
- Prints one line in chat when your pet levels into a rank you can teach it.
- Built-in list of 21 abilities and 84 beasts from public Classic data. Some ranks don't have a beast yet.
